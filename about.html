🌾 About AgriAssist AI

A smart agricultural assistance platform designed to help farmers access useful information and AI-powered crop guidance in one place.

🌱 Smart Agriculture with AI

AgriAssist AI combines modern web technology and Artificial Intelligence to make agricultural information easier to access. Farmers can explore crop information, weather updates, government schemes, market information, soil health tips and AI-powered crop disease guidance from one platform.

🤖 How AI Helps Farmers

🧠 AI Crop Disease Detection

The AI feature helps analyze crop symptoms and provides possible disease information along with causes, treatment suggestions and prevention tips.

🌱 Crop Analysis

Enter the crop name and describe visible symptoms for AI analysis.

🔎 Disease Detection

AI identifies possible crop diseases based on the provided symptoms.

💡 Treatment Guidance

Get general treatment and management suggestions for the identified problem.

🛡️ Prevention

Receive preventive practices to help reduce future crop disease risks.
